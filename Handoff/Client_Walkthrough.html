Website Audit → Rebuild Walkthrough

Prepared 2026-09-14. Covers what was wrong with the current site, what changed, and what we need from you before this can go live.

01 · The Core Problem

The live site has zero path for the buyer who actually drives your revenue.

We fetched and analyzed bigfoot-adventures.com directly. Its navigation (Destinations · Holiday Packages · Deals & Offers · Transport Solutions) has no mention of agents, trade, partners, or ground handling anywhere. A Japanese outbound tour-operator landing on that site today has no idea you do B2B work at all, even though that's about 80% of your revenue.

02 · What We Audited

Full audit across 9 dimensions, severity-tagged.

Information Architecture

No agent-facing pages, hub, or nav path at all.

UX & Conversion

No rate-request flow, no WhatsApp entry, no response-time promise.

Copy

Generic safari-romance language, no capacities, SLAs, or language coverage.

SEO & Analytics

No per-page metadata, no schema, no UTM capture, no way to verify the 3% commission.

Every page a foreign agent needs, plus your existing consumer path.

  • Destinations · Reviews · About / Company Profile · Agent Profile (printable PDF)
  • Request a Rate · Plan a Trip · Nairobi Day Tours · Contact
  • Privacy · Terms · JA / ES / RU draft summaries for your priority source markets
05 · Honesty, By Design

We fixed a real, unsupported claim instead of shipping it.

The old draft's map claimed a "Kenya / Uganda / South Africa" footprint. South Africa isn't East Africa, and nothing in our research supports it. We corrected this to: Kenya confirmed as your core, with Tanzania/Uganda/Rwanda extensions marked "available on request, to confirm" rather than asserted as fact. No invented testimonials, guide names, fleet counts, or partner logos appear anywhere on the new site.

06 · Real Bugs Found & Fixed

We tested it live, not just trusted a clean build.

P0P0P1 ×4

Two deployment-breaking issues, fixed and verified: every internal link and photo was missing the URL prefix needed for GitHub Pages hosting, which would have 404'd on the real deploy, and a critical Next.js security vulnerability affecting Windows dev machines. Full list in Audit/RESULTS.md.

07 · Trust & Attribution

The 3% commission is now actually verifiable.

Every form submission captures UTM source data and logs a structured payload ready for Zoho. Analytics events fire on form submits, WhatsApp clicks, and agent-profile downloads. Before this rebuild, none of this attribution infrastructure existed: the site had no analytics at all.
