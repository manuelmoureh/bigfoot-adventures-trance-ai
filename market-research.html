The Real Engine.

The growth engine here isn't a consumer booking funnel — it's the roughly 15 travel agents who already send business year-round, sourced from India, Japan, Russia, the US, and Spain. Website Audit

The Current Site Sells the Wrong Business
Where It Doesn't Match How Bigfoot Actually Wins Business

What's Working Against the Pivot
The homepage states, verbatim: "Every itinerary is managed in-house by Bigfoot Adventures — no middle agents." That directly contradicts the actual business — travel agents are the money-maker, not something to route around.
No agent-facing rate-request path · No downloadable fleet/guide spec sheet · No dedicated trade/B2B section · Contact form treats every inquiry as consumer-only
Recommended New Site
  • New "Travel Trade / Partners" section with a rate-request flow (not public pricing)
  • Guide showcase built on the Gold/Silver/Bronze tiering
  • Fleet page reframed around reliability and terrain capability for agents booking on their client's behalf
  • Keep destinations, deals, and FAQ content — those are already solid and don't need rebuilding
