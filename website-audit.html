Critical Finding

The Homepage Is Selling Dead Offers

The "Amazing Deals" section on the homepage features exactly two offers. Both are marked Expired on the deals page itself:

2 Nights Entim Mara Platinum Getaway
23% off — valid until Nov 30, 2025 (expired)
2 Nights High-End Ilkeliani Mara Camp
48% off — valid until Nov 15, 2025 (expired)
Why it matters
A visitor who clicks through on a 48% discount that's no longer honored is a trust problem, not just a stale-content one — and it's the first thing "Don't miss out on these incredible deals" shows every visitor.
Fix
Pull expired deals immediately, add an expiry check so this can't recur silently.

Structural Finding

Every Path on the Site Assumes a Consumer

Homepage messaging
States outright: "no middle agents" — directly at odds with agents being the main revenue driver.
Contact form
One generic form, free-text "Subject" field. No way to indicate "I'm booking on behalf of a client."
Transport quote form
Has a Service Type dropdown (Airport/Day Tour/Corporate/Group) — but nothing for trade or agent bookings.
Also inconsistent: a WhatsApp link appears on the Transport page but not on Contact Us — the fastest channel isn't reliably offered.
